FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. There are no paid tiers, limits or watermarks — you host it yourself.

Why does a video fail?

Private, members-only or age-restricted videos need a login. The server owner can supply a cookies file (see README). Live streams in progress aren't supported.

Can I download playlists?

One video at a time for now — paste the individual video link.

Is downloading allowed?

Only download content you own or have permission to save, and respect each platform's terms and local copyright law.
